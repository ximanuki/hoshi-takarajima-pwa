# SEALED — Hidden pricing rules and quirks: print shop dataset

Generator: `generator.py` (seed 7310429). Rows in quotes.csv: 2400. This file is generated by the generator from the same constants it prices with.

## 0. Order of operations (per quote)

1. Pick production **method**: 名刺 → table pricing (`card`); 封筒 → small offset (`env`); 冊子 → on-demand (`od`) if copies < 300 else `offset`; チラシ/リーフレット → `od` if quantity-per-kind < 1,000 else `offset`. The product name チラシ vs リーフレット does not matter; the `finishing` field does.
2. Sum production components (tables of the **price period** of the quote date) → `base`.
3. `after_rate = base × customer rate` (rate in force on the quote date).
4. **Minimum charge**: `after_min = max(after_rate, MIN)` — *not applied to 名刺*.
5. **特急** surcharge: +25% of `after_min`, only if notes contain the literal `特急` (`至急`, `急ぎ`, `お急ぎ` do NOT trigger).
6. Note-triggered flat **extras** (not multiplied by the customer rate): see §4.
7. `pre_round = after_min + rush + extras`; `sub = floor(pre_round / U) × U`, U = 10 yen before 2024-07-01, 100 yen from 2024-07-01.
8. **Delivery** fee by area band, decided on `sub` (§5), added after rounding.
9. **Rep habit**: if rep is 高橋 and `sub + delivery ≥ 10,000`, the total is floored to a multiple of 500.
10. `net` = pre-tax net. Tax presentation (§6) turns it into the written amount.

## 2. Production components

### 2a. Offset flat (チラシ/リーフレット, qty ≥ 1,000)
- Press sheet = 半裁 (half of a full sheet). Up-count: A3 2, A4 4, A5 8 (菊判 family); B4 4, B5 8 (四六判 family).
- 通し `ts = ceil(qty / up)` per kind. Plates per kind = front colours + back colours.
- 版代 = plate price × plates × kinds.
- 印刷代 = plates × kinds × (imp_base + imp_add × (ceil(ts/1000) − 1)).
- 用紙: press sheets = kinds × (ts + 30 × plates_per_kind + ceil(2% × ts)); full sheets = ceil(press/2); **billed in packs of 250 full sheets** (ceil to 250); × price per full sheet (菊 for A sizes, 四六 for B sizes).
- 断裁 flat per job.

### 2b. On-demand flat (qty < 1,000)
- SRA3 up-count: A3 1, A4 2, A5 4, B4 1, B5 2. Sheets S = kinds × ceil(qty/up); paper = (S + max(3, ceil(3%·S))) × OD sheet price.
- Clicks on sides = S × (2 if back colours > 0 else 1). **Marginal tiers**: sides 1–300 at 100%, 301–1,000 at 80%, >1,000 at 60%. Colour click if front colours = 4, else mono.
- データ処理料 1,500 per kind, **waived if notes contain `前回同様` or `リピート`**.
- 断裁 flat per job.

### 2c. Finishing (flat products, either method)
- 二つ折: 2,000 + 1,200 × ceil(pieces/1000). 三つ折 / Z折: 2,500 + 1,600 × ceil(pieces/1000). (pieces = qty × kinds; no revision.)
- 片面PP: 3,000 + per-A4-equivalent price × A4eq × pieces (A3 2, A4 1, A5 0.5, B4 1.5, B5 0.75).

### 2d. 冊子 (中綴じ)
- OD (< 300 copies): SRA3 sheets per copy = pages/4 (A4) or pages/8 (A5); sides = 2 × sheets; same click tiers/paper/data fee as 2b (data fee once per job).
- Offset: A4 → 8 pages per full 台, A5 → 16. full = pages // per_台; a remaining half-台 is work-and-turn. Plates = full × (front+back) + half × front. Impression per plate = imp_base + imp_add × (ceil(copies/1000) − 1). Press sheets = full × copies + half × ceil(copies/2) + 30 × plates + ceil(2%·net); full sheets = ceil(press/2), packs of 250; 菊 price.
- 製本 = setup + per-copy (≤16 pages small rate, >16 pages large rate). No separate cutting.

### 2e. 封筒
- Envelope stock sold by **box** (長3/洋長3 1,000 per box; 角2 500 per box): ceil(qty × kinds / box) × box price. No spoilage charged.
- Plates = env plate × colours × kinds. Impressions per colour per kind = env_base + env_add × (ceil(qty/1000) − 1); **角2 × 1.5**.

### 2f. 名刺
- Per person: first box (100 cards) price by colours + additional-box price × (boxes − 1); special papers (ヴァンヌーボ195kg, パール紙180kg) +300 per box; 片面PP +600 per box; 角丸 +500 per person.
- **Step discount by person index**: persons 1–5 at 100%, 6–10 at 90%, 11+ at 80%.
- No minimum charge.

## 5. Delivery bands

- 引取: 0. 市内: 800, **free if sub ≥ 10,000**. 市外: 1,500, **free if sub ≥ 30,000**.
- 県外: weight-based — weight = pieces × area × g/m² (envelopes: fixed grams each; booklets: copies × pages/2 leaves). ≤ 1 kg → mail rate (520; P2+ 600); else parcel boxes = ceil(kg / 15) × box rate (1,300; P2+ 1,500).

## 6. Tax presentation

- Before 2023-10-01 (pre-invoice template, no tax column): C/D customers → amount is **net**, label `税抜` or blank; G/K/一般 → amount is **gross** = net + floor(net×10%), label `税込` or blank. **A blank label therefore means net for C/D but gross for G/K/一般.** Rep 鈴木 never wrote a label; others left it blank ~30–45% of the time.
- From 2023-10-01 (invoice template): C/D/G → net, label `税抜`, tax_amount = floor(net×10%); K/一般 → gross, label `税込`, tax_amount = floor(gross×10/110) (内税).

## 9. Human quirks (labelled in truth.csv `quirk`)

`rule_net` = the rules above applied to the observable fields exactly as recorded in quotes.csv. `true_net` = the pre-tax net the shop actually meant to quote (after the quirk, before clerical typos). `written_net` = net implied by the written amount and label (blank label read per §6).

|---|---|---|
| clean | 2245 | Priced by the rules. |
| stale_price_table | 24 | Within 60 days after a revision, the rep used the previous period's whole table (incl. its rounding/minimum). |
| stale_customer_rate | 5 | Customer's rate changed (C003 2024-01-01, D02 2024-07-01) but the old rate was used. |
| copied_customer_rate | 12 | Quote copied from the same rep's previous sheet for another customer; that customer's rate stayed. |
| undocumented_discount | 18 | Manager discount with no note: floor to 1,000, −5% (floor 100), −1,000/2,000/3,000, or x,800 price point. |
